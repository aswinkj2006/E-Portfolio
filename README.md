## 🏛️ Design Philosophy: *The Kintsugi Odyssey*

This portfolio is an interactive digital experience built at the intersection of **modern AI engineering** and **classical aesthetic philosophies**.

- **Kintsugi (金継ぎ) Visual Language**: Inspired by the traditional Japanese philosophy of treating fractures and repairs as illuminated history rather than flaws. Seamless SVG crack paths animate across the viewport and heal with molten liquid gold ribbons as the user navigates through the narrative.
- **Stoic Dark Glassmorphism**: Engineered on a deep obsidian palette (`#040814`) with high-opacity frosted glass panels (`rgba(7, 12, 24, 0.88)`), 28px saturation backdrop blurs, and multi-layered elevation shadows to provide uncompromising legibility and depth.
- **Algorithmic Matrix Rain**: A hardware-accelerated HTML5 Canvas engine that renders cascading stoic Greek typography and matrix telemetry that gracefully dissolves upon scrolling.
- **Context-Aware Dynamic Navigation**: An adaptive floating navbar powered by Framer Motion that remains hidden during the hero narrative and slides into view upon scrolling, featuring live section tracking and instant communication channels.
- **Curated Typography**: Classical editorial titles rendered in **Cormorant Garamond** paired with utilitarian, high-contrast monospace and sans-serif typography in **Inter**.
